#All Eyes on Congo

An ongoing history of exploitation, suffering, and injustice

Around 2000 Congolese workers
die from cobalt mining accidents annually

40,000 of the estimated 255,000
mine workers in Congo are children

On average, the workers are paid less than
$2 per day for their labor

Many would consider the resource-rich land of Congo a blessing. But to the Congolese, the abundance of cobalt and other minerals is a curse. With the rising demand for cobalt, the relentless extraction of resources is only worsening, and the people of Congo are suffering more than ever. Workers are left in inhumane conditions that directly imapct their wellbeing, and are severly underpaid for their labor. The human rights violations that are currently occuring in Congo must be stopped.

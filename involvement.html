Take Action

Let the voices be heard. Get involved. Make a difference.

Boycott

By boycotting corporations that have been accused of being complicit, we can put pressure on them to take action and find an ethical source for their materials. Refrain from purchasing new items from the list. Choosing to purchase secondhand devices is a great alternative. Some companies to avoid include Apple, Samsung, Dell, and Microsoft.

Share

Share news and information about the crisis in Congo. Follow users online who advocate for Congo, share their videos, repost content that raises awareness, or even create your own post. Use hashtags such as #freecongo or #congoisbleeding to make a movement and let the issue be known.
